Spelling Mic Test

A 10-word check of whether this device can hear your kids spell letter by letter. Run it once with each kid on their own tablet before we build the full app.

Device check

How to run it

  1. Bee mode runs the real routine: say the word, spell it, say the word again. Saying the word at the end locks in the answer, and the app checks it about 2 seconds after your kid stops talking (no need to tap Done). Saying “can I start over” restarts the spelling, but as in the real bee, letters already said can't change. Uses the same Worker settings as Mode E.
  2. Mode E records the whole spelling and sends it to Google when you tap Done. Letters appear 1–2 seconds later, not while spelling. Enter the Worker URL and passcode once per device.
  3. On a Fire tablet (Silk), use Mode E (or Mode C). It loads a speech model the first time, which takes a minute or two on Wi-Fi. Wait for the green "Speech model ready" before starting.
  4. Pick the word set for your kid. Tap Hear word.
  5. Tap Start spelling. Your kid says the letters at a normal bee pace, then you tap Done.
  6. Coach your kid to leave a short pause between letters. When letters run together, Google tends to hear a word (like “Angie”) instead.
  7. After a longer pause the mic restarts. The dot on the button turns amber (“Wait for red…”). Keep spelling once it's red again, or letters get lost.
  8. Tap what your kid actually said: Spelled it right or Spelled it wrong. That's the answer key the app is graded against.
  9. On 3 of the 10 words, have your kid misspell the word on purpose (swap b/d, drop a double letter). This checks that the app catches mistakes.
  10. When you're done, tap Copy results and paste them into the chat with Claude.
