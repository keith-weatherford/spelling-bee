Spelling Mic Test

A 10-word check of whether this device can hear your kids spell letter by letter. Run it once with each kid on their own tablet before we build the full app.

Device check

How to run it

  1. On a Fire tablet (Silk), use Mode C. It loads a speech model the first time, which takes a minute or two on Wi-Fi. Wait for the green "Speech model ready" before starting.
  2. Pick the word set for your kid. Tap Hear word.
  3. Tap Start spelling. Your kid says the letters at a normal bee pace, then you tap Done.
  4. Coach your kid to leave a short pause between letters. When letters run together, Google tends to hear a word (like “Angie”) instead.
  5. Tap what your kid actually said: Spelled it right or Spelled it wrong. That's the answer key the app is graded against.
  6. On 3 of the 10 words, have your kid misspell the word on purpose (swap b/d, drop a double letter). This checks that the app catches mistakes.
  7. When you're done, tap Copy results and paste them into the chat with Claude.
